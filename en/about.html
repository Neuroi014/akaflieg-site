Purpose

We want SFC to have a place where students teach each other the basics of aeronautical engineering (mechanics, aerodynamics, structures and materials), using aircraft design and construction as the subject.

We draw, we build with our hands, and we fly what we built to check it. Going back and forth between these is how we build knowledge and skill. Beyond design and construction, we also learn by running the group, doing its publicity and its design work.

The name

Akaflieg is short for the German Akademische Fliegergruppe, an academic flying group. These student groups began at German technical universities in the 1920s, and for a century their students have researched, designed and built aircraft themselves.

We follow that idea and share its motto, "Forschen. Bauen. Fliegen." (Research. Build. Fly.) For us, flying means flying model aircraft.

Our policy on full-size aircraft

Flying full-size, crewed aircraft is not part of our activities. This year our work on full-size aircraft is limited to studying the literature on existing gliders and conceptual design exercises.

We will consider building a full-size aircraft only when funding, a workshop and safety procedures are in place and we can plan it ourselves, after filing the necessary notices. An aircraft we build will not be flown as part of our activities.
