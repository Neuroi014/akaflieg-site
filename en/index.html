Akaflieg Keio, founded 1 April 2026

We design aircraft, build them, and fly them to see if we were right.

Akaflieg Keio is a student group at Keio University Shonan Fujisawa Campus (SFC). We teach each other the basics of aeronautical engineering by designing and building aircraft. We are registered with the university as a student group in its non-recognized category.

The name Akaflieg is short for the German Akademische Fliegergruppe, the student groups at German technical universities that design and build their own aircraft. More about the name

You do not need any aviation background. People who want to help with publicity, design or running the group are welcome too.

What we do

  • Workshops

    Forces on a wing, centre of gravity and stability, structures and materials. Members take turns teaching the basics through lectures and exercises.

  • Design and build

    Each member designs and builds a model glider. We check the design by measuring weight and centre of gravity and by load-testing the wing.

  • Flying

    We fly the models off campus, at sites where model flying is permitted, following our safety procedures and the law.
