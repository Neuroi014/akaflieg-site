This site is created as a course exercise.

Join

You do not need any aviation background. Besides design and construction, people who want to help with publicity, design or running the group are welcome.

How to join

  1. Join our Discord

    Join our Discord server. It is where we talk and plan.
    Open the Discord invite

  2. Ask us

    Ask anything about what we do and how we work.

  3. Become a member

    The representative will guide you through the steps.

  4. Take the safety session

    Learn how to handle the tools and materials, then start building with us.

Where, when and fees

Who can joinStudents at SFC
WhereMeetings and workshops: group study rooms in the Media Center, Kamoike Lounge
Design and building: the Media Center fab space and similar facilities
Flying: off campus, at sites where model flying is permitted
ScheduleArranged on Discord.
Fees500 yen a month, collected as 3,000 yen every six months. To be introduced after approval at a general meeting.
ContactDiscord. We mostly work asynchronously, so the time you must be present is short.

Questions

Can I join without knowing anything about aviation?
Yes. We learn the basics together in the workshops.
Can I combine it with other clubs and my studies?
Yes, and we welcome it. Most communication is on Discord, and we do not meet often.
Will I ride in or fly a real aircraft?
No. We fly model aircraft only. See our policy on full-size aircraft.
Do you fly on campus?
No. We fly off campus, at sites whose rules allow it.

Thinking about joining?

No aviation background is needed. Ask us anything on Discord first.
